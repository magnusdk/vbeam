Summary & Recommendations

Key Findings

  • Best overall forward performance: GeneralFastLinearND and FastLinearND — consistently fastest in forward pass across all scenarios.
  • Best backward in loops (unroll=True): OptimizedLinearND — XLA can fuse scatter-adds across unrolled iterations, giving 3x speedup over GeneralFast in ray tracing backward.
  • Best backward in loops (unroll=1): LinearNDFast — its per-dimension lo/hi gather pattern works well even without unrolling.
  • Lowest memory usage (backward): LinearND — zero temp memory in backward pass (in-place scatter-add). GeneralFastLinearND uses ~1× data size; OptimizedLinearND uses ~2× data size.
  • Fastest compilation: LinearNDFast (2D) and LinearND (3D) compile fastest. Quadratic n=3 in 3D is significantly slower to compile.
  • Complex data: GeneralFastLinearND and FastLinearND perform well. OptimizedLinearND is notably slow in backward pass with complex data.
  • Quadratic interpolation (n=3): ~20-40% slower forward, ~33-50% slower backward vs linear (n=2). Compilation time increases significantly in 3D.

Recommendation

  • estimate_h (realistic workload): GeneralFastLinearND and FastLinearND are ~3× faster than LinearND in the full optimization loop.
  • Use GeneralFastLinearND as the default — best forward speed, good backward, supports n_samples=1,2,3, works with standard LinearCoordinate.
  • For ray tracing with gradient optimization (unroll=True): switch to OptimizedLinearND for the interpolator_type — it's 3× faster in the backward pass.
  • No single interpolator wins in every scenario due to fundamentally different XLA optimization patterns in the backward pass.
